# ✂️ Score Stripper

Removes titles, composer lines, page numbers, app credits and other headers and footers from sheet music, so only the music is left.

**▶ Open: https://fenzilla.github.io/score-stripper/**

1. Choose a PDF, PNG or JPG of your sheet music.
2. It finds the music staves on each page and shades everything above and below them in red. Tempo markings and lyrics next to the music are kept.
3. Drag the red ⇕ grips to adjust a page, or tap **Use on all pages**. **Erase a box** covers anything else.
4. Tap **Download clean file** to get `yourfile-clean.pdf` (or `-clean.png` / `-clean.jpg`).

Nothing is uploaded: it all runs in your browser. One file, `index.html`, no build step.
